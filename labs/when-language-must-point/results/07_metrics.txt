'gIoU' in segmentation is a mean of IoUs. 'GIoU' in detection is generalised IoU (Rezatofighi et al.)
  boxes (0, 0, 10, 10) and (5, 0, 15, 10):  IoU 0.333   GIoU +0.333
  boxes (0, 0, 10, 10) and (20, 0, 30, 10):  IoU 0.000   GIoU -0.333
  boxes (0, 0, 10, 10) and (40, 0, 50, 10):  IoU 0.000   GIoU -0.600
  GIoU goes negative for disjoint boxes and keeps falling with distance; IoU stays at 0.
