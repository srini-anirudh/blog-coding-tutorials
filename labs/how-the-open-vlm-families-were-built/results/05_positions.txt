Part 3. One 60 s clip, 448 x 448 frames, two frames per temporal patch. Temporal ids of the video tokens:
sampling  temporal patches  Qwen2-VL time ids  Qwen2.5-VL time ids  Qwen2.5 step
--------  ----------------  -----------------  -------------------  ------------
1 fps                   30              0..29               0..116             4
2 fps                   60              0..59               0..118             2
4 fps                  120             0..119               0..119             1
  Qwen2-VL counts patches, so the same clip gets a different range at each frame rate. Qwen2.5-VL ties the id
  to seconds (2 ids per second), so 60 s always spans about 120 ids.
  A 1-hour video at 1 fps reaches time id 7196; Qwen3-VL instead writes '<t seconds>' as text
  and keeps the time axis small (post: 'large and sparse on long videos').

  Contiguous blocks give time only fast frequencies and width only slow ones. Interleaving gives every axis
  both, which is what the post means by 'each cover both high and low frequencies'.
