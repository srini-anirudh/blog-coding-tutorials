Part 2. A 50:50 mixture in samples, seen as tokens (post: 'Mixtures in tokens')
  short VQA answers  50.0% of samples  ->  0.5% of supervised tokens
  long captions      50.0% of samples  ->  99.5% of supervised tokens

Part 3. One batch with responses of 1, 10 and 200 tokens. Share of the loss gradient each sample gets
normalisation        1 token      10 tokens     200 tokens
-------------  -------------  -------------  -------------
token            0.5% (0.5%)    4.7% (4.7%)  94.8% (94.8%)
sample         33.4% (33.3%)  33.2% (33.3%)  33.4% (33.3%)
sqrt             5.5% (5.5%)  17.2% (17.3%)  77.3% (77.3%)
  Measured from autograd; in brackets the closed form L, 1 or sqrt(L) normalised. They differ slightly because
  the gradient size of each token depends on its own logits. Per-token loss lets the long caption take almost
  all of the gradient; per-sample lets one-word answers weigh as much as captions; sqrt sits between.
