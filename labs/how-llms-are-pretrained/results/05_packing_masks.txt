  as at 30,000, so here it is rounding noise, not a drift that grows with position.

Reading: a document mask alone makes packing exact for the RoPE model, because RoPE depends only on
relative offsets. GPT-2 needs both the mask and the position reset. Resetting positions without a
mask fixes nothing, because tokens still attend to the previous documents.
