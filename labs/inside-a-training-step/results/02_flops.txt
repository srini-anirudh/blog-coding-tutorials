## Freezing. Backward FLOPs relative to the fully trainable model
case                                                   | backward / trainable backward
-------------------------------------------------------+------------------------------
freeze block 1 only (a frozen middle layer)            | 0.8965                       
freeze embeddings + blocks 0-1 (a frozen bottom tower) | 0.5172                       
A frozen middle block still passes dX down, so only its dW GEMMs vanish. A frozen bottom block needs neither, so its whole backward vanishes.

## Recomputation. Total training FLOPs as a multiple of N*D
checkpointing | FLOPs / ND | vs none
--------------+------------+--------
none          | 6.955      | 1      
selective     | 6.955      | 1      
full          | 8.766      | 1.26   
Full recompute reruns each block's forward, but not the LM head, so it lands under 8/6 = 1.33x. It also skips each block's last GEMM (down_proj): torch's checkpoint stops recomputing once every saved tensor is rebuilt, and nothing saves that GEMM's output. tests/test_mechanisms.py checks this FLOP for FLOP.
Selective recompute reruns only elementwise ops, which the counter does not count.
