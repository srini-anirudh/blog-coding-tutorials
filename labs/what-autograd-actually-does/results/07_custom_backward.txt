A square weight is the dangerous case: the wrong gradient has the right shape and training would run.

3. Learn a weight w so that round(w * x) matches round(2.7 * x), 300 SGD steps from w = 0.5:
  true gradient of round      w = 0.500, loss = 42.257, last grad = 0.000
  straight-through estimator  w = 2.700, loss = 0.000, last grad = 0.000
The true gradient of round is zero, so w never moves. The straight-through gradient is not the derivative of
the loss written down, but it points the right way, and w ends near 2.7.
