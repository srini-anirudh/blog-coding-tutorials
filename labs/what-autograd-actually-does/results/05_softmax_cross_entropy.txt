The unfused path breaks before p_true reaches zero. At margin 90, p_true = e^-90 = 8e-40 is a subnormal
float32 number: the loss (90) is still right, but dL/dp = -1/p overflows to -inf, and the softmax
VJP turns into inf - inf = nan. At margin 110, p_true is 0 and the loss is inf as well.
float16 breaks first, at margin 20. float32 and bfloat16 share an exponent range and break together.
The fused rule returns p - y every time.
