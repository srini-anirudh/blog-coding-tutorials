2. Invariance in the forward pass shows up as a projection in the backward pass:
gradient                 largest |dot with 1| per row  largest |dot with x_hat| per row
-----------------------  ----------------------------  --------------------------------
LayerNorm dx, eps=1e-05  8.9e-16                       2.1e-04
RMSNorm dx, eps=1e-05    6.9e+00                       1.4e-04
LayerNorm dx, eps=0      8.9e-16                       1.6e-15
RMSNorm dx, eps=0        6.9e+00                       1.3e-15
Softmax dz               1.2e-16                       -
LayerNorm ignores shifts and scales of x, so dx has no component along 1 or x_hat.
RMSNorm ignores only scale, so dx is orthogonal to x_hat but not to 1.
Softmax ignores a constant added to every logit, so every row of dz sums to zero.
The x_hat projection is exact only at eps = 0. With eps > 0 the layer is not quite scale-invariant, and the
dot product is small but not zero. The shift invariance of LayerNorm does not depend on eps.

3. GELU derivative: zero at x = -0.752 (where GELU itself is lowest), negative below that, and lowest
