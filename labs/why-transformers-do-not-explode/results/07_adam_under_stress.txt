beta2 = 0.999: the quiet stretch steps about 100x under the beta2 = 0.95 run, because sqrt(v_hat) still
remembers the loud start. The burst overshoots lr only modestly here (peak 1.18x), because that same memory
of the hot start keeps the denominator large. Section 3 removes the hot start and shows the clean overshoot.
Warmup only changes the first 300 steps. eps = 1e-2 does nothing at beta2 = 0.999 (the remembered denominator
is far above eps) and halves the quiet-stretch steps at beta2 = 0.95, where sqrt(v_hat) is about 0.01.

The first step is (1 - beta1) / sqrt(1 - beta2) times lr: 3.2x at beta2 = 0.999. The peak, 12 steps later, is 6.5x: m_hat fills in ~10 steps while v_hat has only seen 12 of its ~1,000-step window.

5. Scale invariance: W feeds a LayerNorm, so L(cW) = L(W) but ||dL/dW|| scales like 1/c.

The effective learning rate on W's direction is lr / ||W||^2, so weight decay, which shrinks ||W||, raises it.
