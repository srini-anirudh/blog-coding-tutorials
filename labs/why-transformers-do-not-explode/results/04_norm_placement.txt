With Xavier init (Xiong et al.'s setting) Post-LN puts more gradient on the top blocks, because every LN on
the identity path divides the gradient by the norm of x + F(x). With N(0, 0.02) init the branches start near
zero, x + F(x) is about x, and the Post-LN profile is flat. Pre-LN puts more gradient on the bottom blocks
under either init: the imbalance the post attributes to Shleifer et al.
