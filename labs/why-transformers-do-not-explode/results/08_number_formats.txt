and BF16 rows are eps (2^-23, 2^-10, 2^-7); the two FP8 rows are eps / 2, the unit roundoff.

Without a master copy every update rounds away and the loss stays at its starting value. With an FP32
master copy the 16-bit weights follow the FP32 run; the small gap is the 16-bit rounding of the weights.
