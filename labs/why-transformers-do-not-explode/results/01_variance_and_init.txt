4. Criticality is not enough: 64 critical linear networks (W ~ N(0, 1/n), mean gain exactly 1).

  512   50      -0.0926      0.4592                0.4393
  512  100      -0.1728      0.6131                0.6213
s is the per-layer std of the log-gain, measured from the same runs. Narrower layers jitter more.
The mean is not zero: E[ln gain] is about -1/n per layer even though E[gain] = 1 (Jensen's inequality),
so L = 100 at width 64 should drift by roughly -100/64 = -1.6 in ln-variance (the table shows the measured value).
