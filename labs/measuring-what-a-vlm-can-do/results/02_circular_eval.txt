1500 simulated questions, 4 options, gold letter uniform. Exact chance under CircularEval: (1/4)^4 = 0.0039
model                                   single pass  CircularEval
uniform guesser                               0.224         0.003
always 'A' (position bias)                    0.249         0.000
knows 40%, guesses the rest                   0.539         0.383
knows 40%, picks 'A' otherwise                0.551         0.398
knows 70%, guesses the rest                   0.787         0.707
knows 70%, picks 'A' otherwise                0.775         0.697

A model that knows k of the answers keeps about k under CircularEval, plus (1/4)^4 of the rest
when it guesses. A pure position bias scores about 1/4 on one pass and exactly 0 under CircularEval.
