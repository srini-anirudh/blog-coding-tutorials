== Does changing the future change the past? ==
causal         logits at positions 0..19 after rewriting 20..31, max |difference|: 0.0e+00
bidirectional  logits at positions 0..19 after rewriting 20..31, max |difference|: 1.3e-01

== A KV cache is exact only under the causal mask ==
causal         cached decoding vs a full forward pass, max |logit difference| over 16 steps: 3.0e-07
bidirectional  cached decoding vs a full forward pass, max |logit difference| over 16 steps: 4.3e-01
bidirectional: when token t+1 arrives, every earlier key and value would change, so there is nothing to keep
