== A convolution is translation-equivariant by construction ==
conv(shift(x, 1)) vs shift(conv(x), 1), max |difference|: 0.0e+00
conv(shift(x, 3)) vs shift(conv(x), 3), max |difference|: 0.0e+00
conv(shift(x, 4)) vs shift(conv(x), 4), max |difference|: 0.0e+00

== A ViT is not: only whole-patch shifts, only with no position table ==
ViT, patch 4, mean pooling, random weights. Relative change of the logits when the image is shifted:
  pos=none     shift 1: 5.0e-01   shift 3: 5.7e-01   shift 4: 9.8e-08   shift 8: 1.0e-07
  pos=learned  shift 1: 3.9e-01   shift 3: 3.4e-01   shift 4: 1.0e-01   shift 8: 7.4e-02

== Receptive field of the first layer ==
3x3 conv: output pixel (16, 16) depends on 9 of 1,024 input pixels
one ViT block: the token of one patch depends on 1024 of 1,024 pixels
