== Inception: the 1x1 bottleneck ==
5x5 branch, 480 -> 128 channels: 1,536,000 weights; with a 1x1 projection to 32 first: 117,760 (13.0x fewer)
module 3a at 28x28: naive 0.39 M params, 303 M MACs, 416 out channels; shipped 0.16 M params, 128 M MACs, 256 out channels -> 2.4x fewer MACs
module 4a at 14x14: naive 1.57 M params, 307 M MACs, 928 out channels; shipped 0.38 M params, 74 M MACs, 512 out channels -> 4.2x fewer MACs
the naive pool branch passes all input channels through, so naive outputs are wider as well as costlier.

== EfficientNet compound scaling ==
alpha * beta^2 * gamma^2 = 1.920 (target 2)
phi  depth  width  res   GMACs  ratio to B0  (a b^2 g^2)^phi
  0   1.00   1.00  224    0.39         1.00             1.00
  1   1.20   1.10  258    0.99         2.57             1.92
  2   1.44   1.21  296    1.81         4.69             3.69
  3   1.73   1.33  341    3.25         8.43             7.08
  4   2.07   1.46  392    6.32        16.38            13.60
one axis at a time, phi=1: depth 1.2 -> 1.48x (blocks 16 -> 23), width 1.1 -> 1.15x (formula 1.21), resolution 258 -> 1.49x (formula 1.32)
depth drifts because repeats round up to whole blocks and B0's stages have 1 to 4 blocks. Resolution drifts because every stride-2 layer rounds up: 258 -> 129 -> 65 -> 33 -> 17 -> 9, so the last map is 9x9, not 8.1x8.1. Width rounds to multiples of 8 and drifts down.
