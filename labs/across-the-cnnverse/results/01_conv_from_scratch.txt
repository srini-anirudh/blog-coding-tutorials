== What a filter sees ==
    kernel    sum  flat-region response  edge response (max |y|)
  identity   1.00                 1.000                    1.000
   sobel_x   0.00                 0.000                    4.000
   sobel_y   0.00                 0.000                    0.000
 laplacian   0.00                 0.000                    1.000
  box_blur   1.00                 1.000                    1.000
  gaussian   1.00                 1.000                    1.000
   sharpen   1.00                 1.000                    2.000
    emboss   1.00                 1.000                    4.000
kernels that sum to zero are blind to flat regions; blurs sum to one and pass them unchanged
sobel_x on a vertical edge 4.0, on a horizontal edge 0.0; rotate it 90 degrees and it equals sobel_y up to sign: True
doubling every weight doubles the response: True
template matching: cosine(sobel_x, sobel_x) = 1.000, cosine(sobel_x, a step-edge patch) = 0.471, cosine(sobel_x, the reversed edge) = -0.667
