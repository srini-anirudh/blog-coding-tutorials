A ratio near 1 means the first layer gets as much gradient as the last. Without BN and with the default
init, the signal vanishes on the way back. He init keeps it within an order of magnitude. With BN, the plain
stack does not lose the gradient at all: it grows toward the input, by orders of magnitude at depth 56 and 110
(BN makes deep plain nets explode at initialisation, as Yang et al. 2019 analyse). The residual stack is the
only one that stays near 1 at every depth. He et al. noted that vanishing gradients do not explain the
degradation of deep plain nets with BN; 06_degradation measures that degradation directly.
