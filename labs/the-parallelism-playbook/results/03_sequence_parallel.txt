Reading it: TP+SP sends the same layer-path bytes as TP (AllReduce = ReduceScatter + AllGather). Plain SP
still saves the gathered full-sequence input of each column-parallel matmul, so part of the saving is lost.
Saving only the shard and gathering again in backward recovers it, at the cost of one extra AllGather per
column-parallel matmul in the backward pass. SP also adds a small AllReduce of layernorm and bias gradients.
