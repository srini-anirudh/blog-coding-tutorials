Costs: forward 2 units, backward 4 units per stage (backward = 2x forward). Interleaved v=2 runs chunks of
half the layers, so 1 + 2 units each. zb splits the backward into B (input grad, 2) and W (weight grad, 2)
and fills idle slots with deferred W; it keeps 1F1B's cap of P - s forwards in flight.
Stash = one microbatch's activations for one device-stage; an interleaved stash is half that size.
