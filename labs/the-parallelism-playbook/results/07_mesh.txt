With TP fastest-varying, every TP group of size <= 8 stays in one node, so its 4 AllReduces per layer
ride the fast fabric. TP 16 forces every TP group across two nodes. DP and PP groups cross nodes, which
is where their infrequent, overlappable traffic belongs.
