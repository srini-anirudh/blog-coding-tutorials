3. Why column first. Row-parallel first leaves partial sums before GELU, and GELU does not distribute over +
   GELU applied to partial sums, then summed: max error vs GELU of the full sum = 0.754
   column then row: error 6.0e-08; forward collectives: all_reduce/fwd x1 (8192 B sent)
   row then column: error 2.4e-07; forward collectives: all_gather/fwd x1 (4096 B sent), all_reduce/fwd x1 (32768 B sent)
   Both are correct. Row-first needs two collectives in the MLP forward pass and its AllReduce carries the
   4h-wide hidden activation, four times the bytes of column-first's single AllReduce.
