## 6. Shampoo: vec(L^-1/4 G R^-1/4) as a Kronecker preconditioner, G is 6 x 4
|vec(L^-1/4 G R^-1/4) - (L kron R)^-0.5 vec(G)| / |.| = 9.04e-01
|vec(L^-1/4 G R^-1/4) - (L kron R)^-0.25 vec(G)| / |.| = 1.55e-15
so the quarter powers give (L kron R)^-1/4 = (L^1/2 kron R^1/2)^-1/2: Adagrad's inverse square root
of the Kronecker estimate L^1/2 kron R^1/2 (each of L and R already holds one factor of g^2).
