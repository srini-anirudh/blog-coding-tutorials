4. Attention sinks. One query, 8 keys. Token 0 is the head's dumping ground (logit 6, value 0);
   the other 7 keys are irrelevant (logits near 0, random values).
                       | weights sum | ||output|| | ||output|| after evicting token 0
-----------------------+-------------+------------+----------------------------------
plain softmax          | 1.000       | 0.021      | 1.289                            
softmax + sink logit 6 | 0.019       | 0.021      | 0.021                            
   Without a sink, evicting the first token (streaming) removes the dumping ground and the 'say nothing'
   output becomes an average of irrelevant values. With a learned sink logit, mass leaves the sequence
   and eviction changes nothing important.
