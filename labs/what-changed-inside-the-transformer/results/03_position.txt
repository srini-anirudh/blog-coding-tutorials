1. <R_m q, R_n k> depends only on m - n. Same q, k (d_h = 128, base 1e4), different absolute positions:
m    | n    | m - n | logit    
-----+------+-------+----------
5    | 2    | 3     | +1.908099
103  | 100  | 3     | +1.908090
4003 | 4000 | 3     | +1.908000
3    | 0    | 3     | +1.908099
10   | 2    | 8     | +1.624739
2    | 10   | -8    | +6.813015
The first four rows share an offset of 3 and give the same logit up to float32 rounding.

2. The base is a context-length dial. Wavelength of pair i = 2 pi b^(2i/d), d_h = 128.
model                 | base b | fastest pair | slowest pair | 2 pi b    | pairs > 4k tokens | pairs > 8k tokens
----------------------+--------+--------------+--------------+-----------+-------------------+------------------
Llama 2               | 1e+04  | 6.28         | 54,410       | 62,832    | 18                | 14               
Llama 3 / Olmo 3      | 5e+05  | 6.28         | 2,559,196    | 3,141,593 | 32                | 29               
Qwen3, Gemma 3 global | 1e+06  | 6.28         | 5,063,256    | 6,283,185 | 33                | 30               
The post rounds the slowest wavelength to 2 pi b. With d_h = 128 the exact slowest pair is 2 pi b^(126/128).
A pair whose wavelength is longer than the training context never completes a turn during training;
those are the dials that YaRN and NTK scaling stretch and leave the fast ones alone.

3. NoPE: the causal mask alone carries position. One random attention head, no position signal,
random byte embeddings (d = 64, T = 64, 64 sequences). A ridge probe reads log(1 + position) from the output.
mask          | max |f(Px) - P f(x)| | probe R^2 for log(1+pos)
--------------+----------------------+-------------------------
bidirectional | 1.78e-15             | -0.146                  
causal        | 4.29e+00             | 0.471                   
Without a mask, shuffling the input shuffles the output exactly: position is invisible.
With the causal mask, token m averages over m+1 values, so its output carries its position.
