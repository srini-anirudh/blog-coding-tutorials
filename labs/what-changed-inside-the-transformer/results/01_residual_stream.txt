post       every layer ends on a norm, so ||x_l|| is pinned at sqrt(d) = 11.3 (RMSNorm gain 1 at init).
sandwich and postbranch normalize each branch output, so every write has norm sqrt(d) at init and
||x_l||^2 grows by about d per write: exactly linear in l. The growth rate is set by the gain, which is
why Trinity initializes that gain near 1/sqrt(L).

Part 2. ||dL/dW|| of the FFN down-projection, first block / last block, one batch of Tiny Shakespeare,
mean over 3 seeds. Xiong et al. (2020) predict that Post-LN's last-layer gradient does not shrink
with depth, and Pre-LN's shrinks roughly as 1/sqrt(L). That is why Post-LN needs a warmup and Pre-LN does not.
