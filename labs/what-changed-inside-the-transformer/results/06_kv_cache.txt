   q^T (W_UK c) = -226.2393353600
   (W_UK^T q)^T c = -226.2393353600   (same number, K never built)
   With RoPE on the key: true logit at key position 9          -28.898596
   absorbed matrix built at position 5, used at position 9      -242.292812
   ||R_9 W_UK - W_UK|| / ||W_UK|| = 0.886: the rotation does not pass through W_UK,
   so an absorbed matrix would be needed per position. That is why DeepSeek adds a separate RoPE key.
