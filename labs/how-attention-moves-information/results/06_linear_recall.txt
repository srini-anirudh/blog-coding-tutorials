The softmax cache grows with n and recall stays exact. The linear state stays at d^2 + d floats and
recall falls once n passes a few times d. The positive elu+1 map, on these untrained unit keys,
returns close to the mean value: every phi(k) . phi(k') is large. Learned projections would do better;
this shows the fixed-state limit, not a trained linear-attention model.
