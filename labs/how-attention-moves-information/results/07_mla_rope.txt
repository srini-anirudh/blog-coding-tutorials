Part 1. Fold W_UK into the query (4 heads, dh = 32, latent d_c = 16, 24 tokens)
  no RoPE: max |explicit - absorbed|: 1.07e-14
  RoPE on key: max |true - naive absorbed|: 3.10e+01
  RoPE on key: score scale (std of true scores): 5.72e+00
  Without RoPE, absorption is exact. With RoPE on the full key, the naive absorbed scores are wrong
  by more than the scores' own spread.

Part 2. Could some other fixed matrix stand in for R_delta W_UK?
  best single matrix over offsets 0..127, mean relative error: 0.674
  The rotation depends on the key's position, so no fixed matrix fits. Absorbing it exactly would
  need one matrix per position, which is the same as building the keys.

Part 3. Decoupled RoPE (C3's module): content key from the latent, a small shared RoPE key beside it

  decoupled RoPE, absorbed and cached: max |cached - full|: 3.33e-16
  cached numbers per token (c_kv + k_pe): 24
  With RoPE moved out of the absorbed path, the absorbed and cached forms match the explicit one.
