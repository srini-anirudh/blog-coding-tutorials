XY-cut cuts at the widest empty band. When loose boxes reach across the gutter there is no vertical band to
cut, and it falls back to top-to-bottom order for that region. The learned model sees two boxes at a time.
That is enough to ignore a loose box, but not to learn rules that depend on the rest of the page, such as
where a full-width figure sits between two column bands. LayoutReader reads all blocks at once for this reason.
