Part 1: a 512 x 512 image (Emu3: 4,096 tokens) and a 30-token caption.
  per-token CE, no weight                            image share of loss terms  99.3%
  per-token CE, image x 0.5 (Emu3)                   image share of loss terms  98.6%
  one diffusion mean per image, no weight            image share of loss terms   3.2%
  one diffusion mean per image, x 5 (Transfusion)    image share of loss terms  14.3%
  Per-token counting puts about 99% of the loss terms on the image. A weight of 0.5 trims that share only a little (by the term count alone). One mean per image starts near 3%, and a weight of 5 raises it.
  The two weights correct opposite counting rules. Term counts are not gradient shares, so Part 2 measures gradients.
