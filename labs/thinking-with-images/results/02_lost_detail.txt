Example chain of thought at 448 px:
The bakery closes at 17:00. Here is the text on the signs in the image:

1. The sign on the pink building reads "Bakery."
2. The sign on the green building reads "Bakery."
3. The sign on the purple building reads "Bakery."
4. The sign on the yellow building reads "Bakery."

To determine the closing time, we need to look at the time displayed on the clock on the right side of the image. The clock reads "17:00," which is 5:00 PM in 24-hour format. Therefore, the bakery closes at 17:00.
