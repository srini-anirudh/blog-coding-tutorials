== What freezing saves, FLOPs per parameter per token ==
tower                                                               k                                   made of
------------------------------------------------------------------  -  ----------------------------------------
trained                                                             6  forward 2 + input grad 2 + weight grad 2
frozen, its input needs a gradient (LLM above a trained connector)  4                  forward 2 + input grad 2
frozen, nothing below it trains (encoder at the bottom)             2                                 forward 2
Freezing the LLM in stage 1 saves 0.333 of its compute (a third, not two thirds). scripts/02_measure_k.py measures these k values with a FLOP counter.

The FLOP formula agrees with the paper's direction: the 3B model at 896 px costs more per example than the
