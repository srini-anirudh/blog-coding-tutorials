Part B. Train W with minibatch SGD (batch 64, lr 0.2) for 3000 steps, three weight decays.
angle = mean angular step over the first or last 200 steps. sqrt(2 lr wd) is the predicted equilibrium angle.
      wd  ||W|| start  ||W|| end  angle first  angle last  sqrt(2 lr wd)  loss end  max growth-law error
     0.0        3.938      5.861     2.07e-02    1.03e-02              -    0.3991               2.6e-05
   0.001        3.938      4.473     2.14e-02    1.81e-02       2.00e-02    0.4027                     -
    0.01        3.938      2.660     3.08e-02    5.77e-02       6.32e-02    0.4258                     -

With wd = 0 the norm only grows, and the angular step falls with it: an automatic learning-rate decay.
With wd > 0 the norm stops growing and the angular step settles near sqrt(2 lr wd).
