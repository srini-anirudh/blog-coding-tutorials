Without the norm, entropy collapses towards 0 and, past s = 2, the gradient on the logits falls as s grows.
With QK-Norm, |logit| <= ||q|| ||k|| / sqrt(d) = sqrt(d) = 8 (gain 1). A learned gain can
still sharpen attention, but only by growing the gain itself, which is a parameter you can watch.
