slot utilisation = tokens produced / (slots x decode steps). latency is completion minus arrival.
stalled seq-steps = running sequences x steps they waited on someone else's prefill; chunked prefill
exists to spread exactly this cost. With 8 slots and 8 requests nothing is waiting, so continuous
batching cannot raise utilisation; it still returns short requests early instead of at step 500.
