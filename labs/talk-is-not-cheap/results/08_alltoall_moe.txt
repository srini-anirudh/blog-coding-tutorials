1. Bytes per rank, as a fraction of n, on 4 nodes x 8 GPUs (P = 32). 'network' counts bytes that leave the node.
collective               sent / n  busiest NIC / n  share of bytes leaving node
-----------------------  --------  ---------------  ---------------------------
AllToAll (pairwise)         0.969            0.750                          77%
AllReduce, flat ring        1.938            1.938                          12%
AllReduce, hierarchical     1.938            0.188                          10%
AllToAll sends half of what AllReduce sends, but 3/4 of it must leave the node, and no reordering helps: every
pair really exchanges. The hierarchical AllReduce puts 4x fewer bytes on its busiest NIC than AllToAll does.

2. MoE dispatch: 8 ranks x 8 experts, top-2, 4096 tokens per rank. The slowest receiver sets the AllToAll time.
router skew  busiest / mean rank  with capacity 1.25  tokens dropped
-----------  -------------------  ------------------  --------------
0.0                         1.01                1.01            0.0%
0.25                        1.15                1.15            8.8%
0.5                         1.38                1.30           24.7%
1.0                         1.99                1.54           50.9%
With equal messages the AllToAll cost model holds. As routing skews, the busiest rank receives more than its share
and everyone waits for it. A capacity factor caps that ratio and pays for it in dropped tokens.
