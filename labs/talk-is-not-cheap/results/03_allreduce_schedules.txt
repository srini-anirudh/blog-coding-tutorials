Ring and Rabenseifner sit exactly on the 2(p-1)/p bound. Naive is (p-1)n at the root, tree and recursive doubling
are n log2 p. Rabenseifner also needs only 2 log2 p rounds, the latency floor's order; the ring needs 2(p-1).
