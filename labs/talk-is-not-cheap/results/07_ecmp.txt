The post's ln k / ln ln k is the leading term of an asymptotic result. At k = 64 it gives 2.92; the simulated
busiest uplink carries about 4 flows on average. So the real penalty at this size is worse than the post quotes.
Collisions are near certain at every k shown: the busiest link almost never carries just one flow.
Spraying 1000 packets per flow brings the busiest link to within a few percent of an even share.
Rail-aligned traffic needs no hash at all, so its busiest link carries exactly one flow by construction.
