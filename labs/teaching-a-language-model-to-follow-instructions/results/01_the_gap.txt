Reading: on a bare question this base model usually does answer (see 'gold<=20'): its top next token is a
newline, then the answer. What it does not do is stop; it goes on to repeat the question or write a new one.
Inside a chat template it never saw, it echoes the question and emits junk. So the gap here is turn
structure and stopping more than knowledge, which qualifies the post's 'followed by another question rather
than Paris' picture for this model. These are string checks on greedy decodes of a 135M model, not a
judged evaluation, and one model family.
