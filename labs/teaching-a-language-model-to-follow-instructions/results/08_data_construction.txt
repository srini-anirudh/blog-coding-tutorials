Reading: the kept set grows with k, and so does the number of distinct paths, but more slowly (the rest repeat a path already
kept). The share of kept solutions with a false equation is small, 1% or less to a few percent, and it rests on a handful of
solutions per row (under 10 at k = 1 to 4). Sampling is not bit-reproducible on a GPU: across runs of this script (and its
parser while it was being written) the k = 1 value ranged from 0% to 5% and the k = 16 value from 0.5% to 1.6%. Do not read
a trend into it. The number is rough. The parser only sees plain 'a op b = c' statements; some hits are its own errors (a
sub-expression or algebra it cannot read) and some are real (an arithmetic slip that still ended at the right answer), while
wrong steps that are not arithmetic are invisible to it. It supports 'answer-correct solutions with a wrong step exist', not
a measured rate. One 0.5B model, 200 questions, one seed.
