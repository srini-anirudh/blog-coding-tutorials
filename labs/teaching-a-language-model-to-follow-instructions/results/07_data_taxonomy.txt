D. Contamination: word n-gram overlap of GSM8K test questions with the instruction data (instructions plus responses)
   1319 GSM8K test questions against 66771 Alpaca + Dolly examples
   n = 13: 0.0000 of test questions share an n-gram (0 questions)
   n =  8: 0.0023 of test questions share an n-gram (3 questions)
   n =  5: 0.3177 of test questions share an n-gram (419 questions)
   A planted copy and a light paraphrase of 100 test questions (every 5th word replaced by a different word):
   exact copy        detected at n=13: 100/100, n=8: 100/100, n=5: 100/100;  mean ROUGE-L against the original 1.00
   light paraphrase  detected at n=13: 0/100, n=8: 0/100, n=5: 36/100;  mean ROUGE-L against the original 0.81
   The 13-gram check catches copies and misses the paraphrase that keeps most words. That is the gap the post says embedding-level
   checks must cover; those are not built here.
