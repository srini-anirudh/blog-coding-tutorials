DPO gradient vs -beta * sigmoid(-beta h): max gap 1.1e-16
What to read off the gradient table:
  DPO and SimPO fade smoothly as h grows. SimPO is DPO shifted right by its target margin.
  ORPO's odds term has DPO's shape; it differs in what h measures (log-odds, no reference).
  KTO pushes hardest at its reference point and fades on both sides, so badly wrong examples
  get little push too.
  The two hinges give a constant push and then stop dead: exactly zero past their margin.
  IPO never stops. Past h = 1/(2 beta) = 0.5 its gradient changes sign and pulls
  the margin back. It is the only member that refuses to grow the margin without bound.
