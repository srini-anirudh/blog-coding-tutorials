A. The assistant-only loss, computed three ways, fp32 on CPU, SmolLM2-135M base
   20 examples. Sum of NLL over the masked-in tokens (nats):
     (1) manual: logits, per-token NLL, multiply by the mask        (2) Hugging Face loss with labels = -100
     (3) -log p(prompt + response) minus -log p(prompt), no mask at all
     max |(1) - (2)| = 3.55e-05      max |(1) - (3)| = 1.75e-04   (largest relative difference 2.7e-06)
   Masked tokens are still context. One reply, mean assistant-token NLL: after its own prompt 3.381; after a different prompt 3.529;
   with its own prompt hidden from attention 4.074. The mask changes which terms are summed; it does not change what the model sees.
