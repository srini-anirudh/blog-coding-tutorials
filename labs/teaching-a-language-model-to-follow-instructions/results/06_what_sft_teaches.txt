A. Format adherence against the number of SFT examples (SmolLM2-135M base, Alpaca prompts, 5 trained format constraints)
   Evaluation: 40 held-out prompts x (5 trained constraints, 1 unseen constraint, 1 with no constraint) = 280 prompts, greedy, 160 new tokens.
   'adheres' = the reply ended with <|im_end|> within the budget AND passes the exact checker for its constraint.
   'seen' averages the 5 trained constraints; 'unseen' is 'Begin your answer with the word Sure,' (never in training);
   'stops' is the share of unconstrained prompts whose reply ended with <|im_end|>.
   Training: lr 5e-05, batch 16, about 150 optimiser steps for every N (so small N gets many epochs), responses rewritten to satisfy the constraint.
   The step count is held fixed so that N is the only thing that changes. A first attempt with lr 2e-5 and 48 steps gave no stopping at all for N <= 64.
