Task: running sum of n digits mod 10. Train: 12000 examples with n uniform in 2..12, 2 epochs, lr 0.0001, batch 32, SmolLM2-135M base, assistant-only loss.
Evaluation: 300 fresh questions per n, greedy. n = 16 and 20 are longer than any training example. Chance for the final answer is 0.10.

   Share of all samples with an invalid trace: 0.065.
   'predicted bad share' is what you would see if a wrong trace landed on the right final digit by chance (probability 0.1); it uses
   only the one-sample rate and does not depend on k. In this task the measured share is far below it, because a slip is carried
   forward: every later running total is consistently off by the same amount, so the final digit is wrong unless a second slip
   cancels the first. An outcome filter lets bad reasoning through only where errors can cancel or the answer can be guessed. That
   depends on the task. Here it is about 0.1% of kept samples. Sampling more raises the number kept, and does not change this share
   in any visible way (single model, 200 questions, 3,200 samples).
